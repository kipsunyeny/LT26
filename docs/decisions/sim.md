# Gameplay/sim decisions (agent `sim`)

- **Preset spots.** `fk-centre` (0, 22), `fk-left-d`/`fk-right-d` (∓6, 18.5) just outside the box at the edges of the D, `fk-wide-left`/`fk-wide-right` (∓15, 18.5); `pk-spot` (0, 11); `ls-centre` (0, 26), `ls-left`/`ls-right` (∓9, 25). All free kicks are 16–35 m from the goal centre and outside the penalty area.
- **Free placement snaps to whole metres and stays outside the penalty area.** The key `fk-x{X}-z{Z}` then names exactly one ball position. A request inside the box moves to the nearest valid point, because a direct free kick cannot be taken inside it. `setSpot('fk-x3-z25')` is accepted too.
- **The wall covers the post on the ball's side; a central ball (|x| < 0.5) is walled on the left post.** A right-footer's natural inside-foot bend goes right to left. The outside man stands with his centre on the ball→post-centre line (x = ±3.72). The line is perpendicular to that direction at 9.15 m, spaced 0.5 m and extending towards the goal centre.
- **Wall count comes from the angle the goal subtends:** ≥ 16° → 5, ≥ 12.5° → 4, else 3. That gives 5 for close central kicks, 4 for wide or 30 m kicks and 3 from 35 m.
- **Wall jump is ballistic:** v0 = √(2g·0.4), 0.15 s after the strike, airborne about 0.57 s. The capsules carry the jump velocity for collisions.
- **Free-kick keeper stands 0.8 m from the goal-angle bisector towards the far post, 0.5 m off the line.** He reacts 0.10 s later than normal, because the wall screens his view.
- **Keeper model:** the hands' midpoint moves inside a superellipse envelope |dx/2.9|⁴ + |(y−1)/1.2|⁴ ≤ 1 around his set position, which gives 2.9 m either side and a 2.2 m top. Motion is acceleration-limited: lateral 24 m/s² up to 7.5 m/s, vertical 30 m/s² up to 5 m/s, with quick braking but a full push-off to reverse. A full-stretch dive takes about 0.5 s and a 0.5 m reach about 0.2 s. The feet step up to 0.9 m. The body capsule runs from the hip towards the hands (radius 0.22, 1.45 m + radius). The hands are two 0.12 m spheres. After the reaction he re-predicts the ball every 0.05 s by stepping physics `stepBall` from the live ball state, with no noise and no colliders. That is a prediction, not a gameplay trajectory.
- **Keeper side-steps (≤ 3 m/s) only when the predicted ball is beyond his reach at that height.** A slow, lofted shot to the far corner is therefore saveable, while a penalty (about 0.4 s) cannot use it.
- **Penalty read:** the cue is the side the kick is aimed at: the no-scatter preview crossing, with |x| < 0.5 m counted as "centre". The keeper follows the cue with probability 0.65, otherwise he picks the other side (for a central cue, left or right 50/50). `disguise` inverts the cue (a central disguise reads as right). Before his reaction time the keeper only pre-shifts gently towards his guess (8 m/s², ≤ 1.8 m/s), so a central mid-height shot remains saveable. With the pre-shift at 10 m/s² / 2.2 m/s, 25 % of central shots went in, which failed the ≤ 20 % target.
- **Keeper plan draws:** reaction is uniform in 0.20–0.25 s (seeded). Every strike draws exactly two Gaussians (scatter), one reaction value and one guess value from the sim RNG, so a replay with the same seed and intents is bit-identical.
- **Scatter:** σ = 0 below 90 % power, 1.5° at exactly 90 %, rising to 3.5° at 100 %. It is applied independently to azimuth and elevation. Timing σ combines with it in quadrature. `kick.scatterDeg` reports that combined σ, not the drawn deviation.
- **Timing multiplies `power` (before the km/h mapping), not speed.** This follows ARCHITECTURE §2 ("power multiplier").
- **A strike in `aiming` is accepted in every mode with timingErrorMs = 0.** In a penalty that is a strike without a run-up; in a long shot it strikes the ball at rest. Otherwise a scheme that never calls `startRunUp` could not shoot at all.
- **Long shot push:** speed 3 + 3·power m/s, launched with rolling top spin v/(2πr) so it rolls instead of skidding (PHYSICS.md). The ideal contact is when the taker, chasing at ≤ 7.5 m/s (6 m/s²), reaches his strike position beside the rolling ball. That is computed at the push by running the same deterministic roll and chase forward, and announced as `runUpStart.contactAt`. `startRunUp()` returns it during `pushed`. The taker can only "reach" the ball after it has first rolled 0.5 m ahead of him.
- **Closing defender:** he starts 10 m in front of the ball and 1 m to the goal-centre side, reacts 0.25 s after the push and closes at ≤ 6 m/s. He then holds 2.4 m from the ball, matching its approach, and freezes at the strike. He blocks roughly one half of the goal at low height, so the taker has to go the other way or bend it.
- **Target aim with a moving ball:** the solver is run at kick speed + ball speed along the aim line and treated as the resultant launch. With angles aim the ball velocity is added vectorially.
- **Solver fallback (unreachable target):** aim straight at the target's azimuth, with elevation equal to the line-of-sight angle plus half the vacuum 45° loft term, clamped to −0.1…0.8 rad.
- **Result rules:** a goal is decided the moment the ball centre passes z < −0.11 inside the posts and under the bar. It still counts after touching the frame or the keeper, and no net contact is used. Otherwise the ball is decided when it is caught, crosses the line outside, stops, leaves the area, or has been heading upfield for 0.5 s after any block contact, or at 6 s. Priority: caught > saved > post/bar (first frame touch) > wall/defender > miss.
- **Summary stats:** `apexM` and `lateralCurveM` cover the free flight only (until the first non-ground contact or the line crossing). `timeToGoalS` is the crossing time, else the first blocking contact, else the decision time. `missDistanceM` is taken from the crossing point, else the closest 3D approach to the goal mouth.
- **Event hygiene:** ground contacts are emitted only for bounces (vertical speed > 1 m/s). Same-tag contact events are merged within 0.15 s, so a net or rolling ball does not flood the listeners. `save` is emitted on the first keeper touch, and again as `caught: true` if a later slow touch holds it.
- **Recording:** a `Recording` object is assigned to `state.lastRecording` at the result. Its `frames`/`events` arrays keep growing for the 0.5 s tail, so the UI can start the replay immediately. After the tail the sim keeps stepping the ball physics (ball settles in the net) until `resetShot`.
- **`update(dt)` clamps a frame to 0.1 s.** The sim clock advances in exact 1/240 s steps.
- **Taker:** stands 0.6 m left of and 0.5 m behind the ball relative to the ball→goal-centre direction (mirrored for left-footers). The penalty run-up starts 1.3 m left and 2.2 m back (≈2.55 m) with a smoothstep over 0.9 s.
- **Session log best kick:** goals beat non-goals. Among goals, the smallest distance from the crossing to any of the four inner corners of the mouth wins; among non-goals, the smallest miss. Stored paths are rounded to mm. Invalid entries in storage are dropped individually.
