# Input & UI decisions (agent `ui`)

- **Finger speed is measured over the last 80 ms before release,** not over the whole swipe. A flick is judged by its release speed; a slow wind-up followed by a fast flick is a fast kick. A lift-off with no movement is ignored so it does not dilute the speed.
- **CSS px → metres: 96 CSS px per inch for fine pointers (mouse), 150 CSS px per inch on coarse pointers (`(pointer: coarse)`, touch tablets/phones lay out ≈132–160 CSS px per physical inch).** Fix round: with 96 on tablets a physical flick read ~1.5× too fast. Chosen once per scheme attach.
- **Power ease-in exponent 1.6** (power = f^1.6, f = normalised finger speed 0.3–2.5 m/s). Mid-speed swipes give ~33 % power, so full power needs a real flick.
- **Swipe curvature = signed area between the path and its chord / chord².** Dead zone 0.01; full ±10 rev/s at 0.12 (a circular arc whose sagitta is ~18 % of the chord). The ball curves the way the swipe hooks: a swipe that bows left of its chord (hooks right at the end) gives sideSpin > 0 (curves to +x).
- **Swipe azimuth = the chord direction, read on the pitch.** The screen images of 1 m towards the goal centre and 1 m to the right at the ball (via `worldToScreen`) turn the screen direction into a ground direction; straight up the screen always aims at the goal centre. Swipes more than 80° off that direction, backwards swipes and swipes shorter than 24 px give no kick.
- **Start on the ball → elevation 2° (top) / 11° (centre) / 20° (bottom), top spin up to +5 and back spin up to −5 rev/s.** The top…bottom band is ±max(40 CSS px, 2.2 r) around the ball centre with a ±10 px centre dead band (fix round: a 10–20 px ball is too small to hit its top or bottom exactly). 20° is enough to clear a wall from ~20 m.
- **A swipe must start within max(4 ball radii, 120 px) of the ball centre.** "On or near the ball" while still letting a thumb start slightly below it.
- **`createSwipeScheme({ ballWorld })` takes an optional ball-position provider.** `InputContext` only exposes the ball's screen position, which is not enough to project a ground direction; without the option the scheme falls back to screen-up = goal, screen-right = right.
- **Dial power bar: hold = triangle wave 0→1→0 over 1.6 s, release sets it; a tap (<180 ms, no drag) sets the tapped value exactly.** The tap and the ±1 % arrow keys make the dial repeatable, as the brief requires "everything numeric and repeatable".
- **Knobs step 0.5 rev/s; drag up/right increases (10 px per rev/s for curve, 16 px for dip); arrow keys ±0.5, Home/0 or double-click resets.**
- **Reticle range x ±12 m, height 0–6 m on the goal plane,** so a mis-aim well wide/over is possible. Pressing anywhere on the empty play area moves the reticle there.
- **Gesture plan per mode/phase (`gesturePlan`):** free kick = strike; penalty = the press starts the run-up, the release strikes; long shot = first gesture is a push, the second gesture's press starts the run-up (the app ignores the call if the sim already started one) and its release strikes. Nothing is accepted in flight/result/replay. The release re-checks the mode captured at press and that the current phase still allows that gesture kind (`releaseAllowed`), otherwise nothing is emitted.
- **A run-up the UI started is cancelled (`sim.cancelRunUp()`) when the gesture ends without a strike** (tap, too short/backwards swipe, pointercancel, Shoot cancelled, scheme detached). The long-shot run after a push belongs to the sim and is never cancelled by the UI.
- **Long shot, ball rolling ('pushed'): a tap on/near the ball is a strike** towards the goal centre along the pitch, 11° elevation, no spin, power = the dial's current power (default 0.7).
- **Menu, mode tabs, spot buttons, the disguise toggle and the mini-map ignore presses while a kick gesture is in progress** (`core.ui.gesture`, set by the schemes through an app-side context hook).
- **The penalty "disguise" toggle lives in the HUD and the app adds `disguise: true` to penalty strikes** instead of every scheme knowing about it.
- **The UI keeps its own list of spot keys/labels (`ui/spots.ts`)** because `data/presets.ts` did not exist when the UI was written; positions stay the sim's business (the mini-map shows `sim.state.ballStart`). Free placement is rounded to the metre and clamped to 16–35 m and ≥ 2 m in front of the line.
- **Screens are mounted/unmounted, not hidden,** so every `data-testid` (e.g. `btn-back`) exists once in the DOM at a time.
- **HUD layout mirrors with the kicking foot.** Right-footer: Talilei bottom-left, dial controls bottom-right, set-up panel top-left, shot card top-right. Left-footer: Talilei bottom-right, dial controls bottom-left, shot card top-left (the set-up panel moves top-right on medium/compact screens).
- **Three layout sizes.** Large (≥ 1100 × 700): dial controls in a row. Medium (< 1100 wide or < 700 tall, e.g. 1024×600): dial controls stacked in a 256 px column at the side so they stay clear of the ball, set-up panel horizontal (mini-map beside the spot buttons), and the dial column is hidden while the result card is shown (the card takes its place). Compact (< 900 wide or < 440 tall, e.g. 740×360, 800×500): smaller chrome, hint on one line with ellipsis, notes and last-input line hidden, 64 px Shoot. E2E checks Talilei's corner, the ball, panel overlaps, off-screen and ≥ 44 px targets at 740×360, 800×500, 1024×600, 1280×800 and 1920×1200 (both feet where the brief asks).
- **The shot card is a flex column with a scrolling data list (max-height = viewport − its top offset); the result line and the Next/Replay buttons are always visible.**
- **Edges are padded with `env(safe-area-inset-*)` (viewport-fit=cover).**
- **Title: three grid columns — logo | menu (clamp 280–420 px) | Talilei at full viewport height with his natural width** — so he can never cover the menu at any landscape width ≥ 740 px.
- **Stats/settings/title use the 3D title camera as a dimmed backdrop; the sim only advances on the play screen,** so run-up timing (sim clock) never runs in the background.
- **Replay shows the result, speed/spin, a side-view profile of the recorded path and a progress bar at 0.35×; it holds the last frame and offers "Replay again".** Its ghost is the best kick at the spot from before the replayed shot was recorded (never the shot itself).
- **`audio.unlock()` is called on every pointerdown/pointerup/touchend/click/keydown (it is idempotent and resumes a suspended context).** Fullscreen + landscape lock is attempted once, on the first trusted activation (pointerup/touchend/click/keydown) on a coarse-pointer device, never under automation (`navigator.webdriver`). Portrait shows the rotate prompt (pure CSS media query).
- **Without WebGL a pinhole fallback camera (2.3 m high, 4.5 m behind the ball, 46° FOV, 12° down) provides `worldToScreen`/`screenToGoalPlane`,** so both schemes and the E2E tests work with `scene === null`; the aim preview is then drawn in the SVG overlay.
- **The ball's screen position is exposed as `data-ball-x/y/r` on the input layer** so E2E (and QA) can aim swipes without reaching into the renderer.
- **"Inside/outside of the foot" labels:** right-footer: sideSpin < 0 (curls left) = inside; left-footer mirrored. Physics sign unchanged. Shown on the dial knob, in the last-input line (swipe too) and on the shot card (from the kick's launch side spin).
- **Shot card wording: result `'wall'` in a long shot reads "Blocked by the defender"** (QA D-2; the contract's `ShotResult` has no defender value and long shots have no wall).
- **Aim preview is throttled and cached:** at most one `sim.previewPath` per frame and at most one per 100 ms (≤ 10 Hz), with a 48-entry LRU cache keyed by the quantised aim (1 cm / 0.03°), power (0.5 %), spins (0.05 rev/s), kind, mode, spot, ball position, altitude and foot (fix round: a preview costs 10–19 ms on a tablet).
- **Mini-map snaps to the nearest whole-metre cell that is itself legal (16–35 m, ≥ 2 m out, on the map)** instead of rounding, so a rounded spot can never fall just inside 16 m or beyond 35 m.
- **Dial power bar: a cancelled press restores the power set before it.**
- **The run-up timing mirror (power/scatter formulas) was removed from `input/runUp.ts`;** the rule lives only in the sim. The UI keeps the ±80 ms window for drawing the zone and grading a release.
- **Brand lint:** `tests/unit/ui.brand.test.ts` fails on any hex, rgb/hsl/lab/color() or CSS named colour in `styles.css` other than #3FC9FC, #000E29, #FFFFFF and rgba() of them, and on any colour literal in `src/ui`/`src/input` TypeScript. The danger button is white-on-navy (no red).
- **E2E touch swipes use CDP `Input.dispatchTouchEvent` with explicit timestamps** for the power assertion, so the measured finger speed does not depend on CDP latency on a loaded CI machine.
- **HUD state is synced once at mount and on tab/spot clicks, not only in the animation loop,** so the HUD is complete even when frames are slow (headless SwiftShader measured ~2.7 fps at 1280×800 and <0.5 fps on the DPR-2.25 tablet profile).
- **Input E2E tests wait for the ball's projected position to be stable and on screen before swiping, and use `test.slow()`.** The kick camera is only placed by `scene.render`, so projections are off-screen for the first frames after entering a mode; at sub-1 fps every CDP touch event also waits for a frame.
