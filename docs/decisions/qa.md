# QA decisions

- **Integration tests fly the ball on the sim clock after the real gesture.** The swipe / dial / mouse gesture is real (CDP touch events, mouse), but once the `kick` event is seen the test advances `__lt26.sim.update(0.1)` from `page.evaluate` until `result`. Headless SwiftShader runs at a few fps (below 1 fps on the tablet profile) and the frame loop clamps dt to 0.1 s, so waiting on rAF would cost minutes per kick; the fixed 240 Hz sub-stepping makes the outcome independent of how the time is batched.
- **Penalty / long-shot strikes are released just before the ideal contact.** After the press (run-up) or the push, the test advances the sim to 60–100 ms before `runUpStart.contactAt` and then swipes; the exact timing error is whatever the page measured and is shown on the card, not asserted.
- **Long-shot card speed bound is 115 + 21.6 km/h.** The brief (§1.1) says the moving ball's velocity adds to the kick; the push is at most 6 m/s (21.6 km/h), so a long-shot strike can legitimately exceed the 115 km/h power ceiling. All other modes are checked against 40–115 km/h (with 1e-6 tolerance for float rounding at exactly 40).
- **"Frames match" means 60 Hz from strike to result + 0.5 s.** `lastRecording.frames.length` must equal `floor((t_result − t_kick + 0.5)·60) + 1` within ±2, and every world the scene renders on the replay screen must be one of the recorded frames (proves playback, not re-simulation). The trail check wraps `scene.setTrail` and requires a call with exactly `lastShot.path.length` points.
- **Distribution tests run on the title screen.** The app's frame loop only advances the sim on the play screen, so on the title screen the test owns the sim clock completely; kicks go through `sim.applyIntent` / `startRunUp` exactly as the UI would call them, with `atMs = sim.state.time·1000`. Seeds are not fixed (the app seeds from `Date.now()`), so each run is a fresh sample.
- **Penalty inputs:** "low corner" = dial target x = ±2.9…3.2 m, y = 0.25…0.35 m, power 0.85, timing within ±70 ms, 1 in 5 with disguise; "central mid-height" = x within ±0.3 m, y 1.0–1.4 m, power 0.55–0.85.
- **Free-kick "repeatable curling goal"** = the aim-assist (`sim.solveAim`) launch for a candidate (target near a corner, 75–105 km/h, |side spin| 6–9 rev/s, top spin 0/2) scores with a lateral curve > 0.5 m, and the same kick then scores in at least 2 of 3 repeats (fresh keeper reaction and noise each time). The grid includes 75 km/h and x = ±3.3 m: a first grid without them (85–105 km/h, x = ±3.0/2.6) found nothing from the D edges, which an exhaustive 2,000-kick exploration showed was a search-grid limit, not a product defect.
- **Long-shot timing check compares launch speeds of the same intent at 0 ms and ±250 ms** (≥ 5 km/h lower when late/early), using the `kick` event's launch speed.
- **Perf test renders 100 aiming frames + 200 frames with a curling kick in flight** by calling `scene.render(sim.state.world, 1/60)` in one `page.evaluate`, and gates on the maximum draw calls / triangles over all 300 frames (not the mean). Mean render time is measured around each call and printed but not gated.
- **Visual baselines mask the canvas only where it animates by itself** (title orbit camera, replay orbit camera, post-result ball roll, replay progress bar). Play screens compare the full 3D kick view. The shot-card test freezes `Date.now()` before boot so the sim seed, the keeper and the card numbers are deterministic. Screenshot timeout is 60 s (a stable SwiftShader screenshot at 1280×800 takes > 10 s). Baselines exist per project (chromium and tablet-android), both at a 1280×800 viewport.
- **Unmasked review copies** (`review-*.png`) are written to the test output folder for people to look at; they are not compared.
- **Replay-frame check polls for 3 distinct rendered frames (up to 90 s)** instead of a fixed 3 s wait: on the tablet profile SwiftShader rendered no frame at all in 3 s, which failed the test without any product fault.
- **Defect-documenting tests live in `qa.defects.spec.ts`** and are expected to fail until the defect is fixed (none are skipped).
- **Round 2: the sim clock is held during a CDP gesture that must land at a chosen time.** `freezeSimClock` no-ops `sim.update` (the frame loop keeps rendering) while the penalty / long-shot strike swipe is dispatched, after `runToContactAndFreeze` has run the sim to 20 ms before the ideal contact. Without it each headless CDP event costs up to 0.1 s of sim time and the new run-up abort (contact + 1 s) and long-shot auto-scuff (contact + 0.6 s) fire before the release.
- **Round 2: long-shot "well-placed" kicks aim at the far corner from the closing defender with the longest push.** The shorter push (intentional change) leaves the defender in the line of every near-side shot; that is filed as D-3 with its own failing test rather than hidden by the distribution test.
- **Round 2: animated canvases are hidden, not masked, in the visual baselines.** A `mask` box is painted over the whole page for a full-screen canvas (round-1 baselines were blank). The canvas gets `display: none` and `scene.render` is paused for the screenshot (a hidden SwiftShader canvas otherwise stalls the capture for minutes), then both are restored. All baselines are deleted before regenerating, because `--update-snapshots` keeps a baseline that is within tolerance.
